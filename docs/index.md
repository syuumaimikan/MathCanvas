# MathCanvas: Next-Generation Commercial Math Workspace

MathCanvas is a state-of-the-art cross-platform application seamlessly blending handwriting, 2D/3D dynamic graphing, Computer Algebra Systems (CAS), and interactive physical simulations. Designed for students, educators, engineers, and researchers, MathCanvas redefines how mathematical ideas are visualized and solved.

## Core Pillars
- **Offline-First & Privacy-Focused**: Works entirely locally, ensuring your mathematical ideas and proprietary calculations are never sent to external servers without permission.
- **Native Performance**: Built entirely in Rust (wgpu, egui) for blazingly fast startup times and hardware-accelerated rendering on Windows and Android.
- **Seamless Interoperability**: Smooth transition between keyboard-driven precision math and freehand stylus expressions.
